ai: a faint after standing up fast, heat or the sight of blood is often harmless, but any fainting deserves a same-day medical check to rule out heart causes, especially if it came without warning, during exercise, or with palpitations. sit or lie down now and drink water if you feel it returning.

user: what do i do about severe bleeding?
ai: press hard on the wound with a clean cloth and do not let go, raise the limb above heart level if you can, and call your local emergency number if bleeding soaks through or spurts. keep adding cloths on top rather than removing them. stay with the person and keep them warm.

user: someone is having a seizure, what do i do?
ai: clear the area of hard objects, cushion the head, and time the seizure. do not hold them down or put anything in their mouth. after it ends, roll them onto their side in the recovery position. call emergency services if it lasts more than five minutes, repeats, or it is their first seizure.

user: how do i know a stroke?
ai: remember fast: face drooping on one side, arm weakness or drift, speech slurred or strange, time to call your local emergency number immediately. note when symptoms started, since treatments work within hours. even if symptoms pass, still call, because a mini stroke is a warning.

user: i am having a panic attack
ai: this feels frightening, but it will pass, usually within ten to twenty minutes. breathe out slowly and longer than you breathe in, feel your feet on the floor, and name five things you can see around you. cold water on your wrists or face can help too. if this is new or keeps happening, please talk to a doctor so other causes are ruled out and you get real support.

user: my blood pressure reading was high today
ai: a single high reading can come from stress, caffeine, a full bladder or a cuff issue, so rest five minutes and measure again. if it stays above one forty over ninety, contact your doctor, especially with headaches, chest discomfort or vision changes. call emergency services for readings above one eighty over one twenty with symptoms. keep a log of readings to show your doctor.

user: my baby has a fever. what do i do?
ai: babies under three months with any fever of thirty eight celsius or more need a doctor immediately, no home treatment first. for older babies, fluids in small frequent sips, light clothing, and paracetamol dosed by weight for discomfort help, and a doctor should see any baby who is unusually sleepy, breathing fast, refusing feeds, has a rash that does not fade under a glass, or has had fever more than a day.

user: should i exercise with this injury?
ai: do not train through sharp pain, swelling or instability, since that usually deepens the injury. keep moving the rest of your body gently, and let the injured part rest, ice and elevate as needed. a physiotherapist or doctor should clear you before returning to full training, especially for head injuries, where any concussion symptoms mean stop immediately.
